merge sort:
best case: O(nlogn)
worst case: O(nlogn)
average case: O(nlogn)

quicksort:
best case: O(nlogn)
worst case: O(n^2)
average case: O(nlogn)

key differences:
1. merge requires extra space to split data, makes it not
in place
2. merge sort is stable, but quicksort swap can reorder data
3. performance: merge sort is consistant, but for quick sort
if unluckily picked bad value, it could be in O(n^2)
